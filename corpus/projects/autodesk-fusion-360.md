## Context

Fusion 360 Web is the browser client of Autodesk's CAD product. The work was delivered through Globant for Autodesk, on a product used by professional users rather than internal staff, so interface regressions were visible to customers.

## What I did

Built and maintained features in Fusion 360 Web across two frameworks, AngularJS and React, in the same product surface. The codebase carried both during its migration, so a change often had to land in each of them and stay consistent.

Delivered self-management features for end users, including the Personal Hub Retirement Self Service Window and ADP analytics inside Personal Hub Retirement.

## My role

Frontend development inside a distributed team, on an established product with an existing architecture, release process and quality bar. That means reading unfamiliar code, matching patterns already in place, and shipping changes that survive review.

## What it shows

- Working inside a large legacy framework migration rather than starting greenfield: AngularJS and React coexisting in one product.
- Product work for professional users, where the interface is the product and a regression is a customer-facing defect.
- Contributing to an established codebase and its conventions instead of imposing new ones.

## Stack

AngularJS, React, TypeScript, browser-based CAD client.
